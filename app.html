RruleWhy

Paste an iCalendar RRULE and a start date. RruleWhy lists the dates it really produces, says it in plain words, and points out the traps: the 31st that skips months, the BYSETPOS that picks a different day, the week start that moves dates.
Whole-day rules only: FREQ DAILY, WEEKLY, MONTHLY, YEARLY with INTERVAL, COUNT, UNTIL, WKST, BYMONTH, BYWEEKNO, BYYEARDAY, BYMONTHDAY, BYDAY, BYSETPOS. No hours or minutes, no time zones or daylight saving, no EXDATE or RDATE. Follows RFC 5545 section 3.3.10 (read in full); checked against every day-level example in section 3.8.5.3 that was fetched and against Python dateutil on random rules. Nothing leaves your browser.
