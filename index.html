RruleWhy
"Monthly on the 31st" does not mean the last day of the month.
Open the app
Start on 31 January with FREQ=MONTHLY and you get January, March, May, July: February, April, June are skipped, not moved. Two biweekly rules that differ only in WKST give different dates. RruleWhy shows both.

What it does

  • Lists the next dates an RRULE produces, with weekdays
  • Reads the rule back in plain words
  • Flags rule mistakes the RFC forbids, and traps the RFC leaves open
  • Matches every day-level example fetched from RFC 5545 and Python dateutil on random rules
Follows RFC 5545 section 3.3.10. Static, client-side, nothing sent. Source
